Quick start

  1. Back up your projects and keep a known-good firmware pair. Put the original Roland 5.52 SP404MKII_APP0.bin and the generated SP404MKII_APP1.bin at the root of the update SD card.
  2. Insert the card. Hold SHIFT while powering on, then press VALUE at the update screen. Wait for UPDATE COMPLETE!, then power off, remove the card, and restart. Roland 5.52 update instructions
  3. Press SHIFT + RECORD SETTING to open Sound Generator. Press SHIFT + SUB PAD to enter Sequence Genie. Press Pad 16 to audition.
  4. On either Genie Suite build, press Pad 15 through Setup → Piano Roll → Modes. On Modes, choose Pad 1 for Chord, Pad 2 for Granule, or Pad 4 for Vox. On Legacy, Pad 15 switches between Setup and Piano Roll.

Turn VALUE to browse rows. The highlighted label shows the selected row. Push to highlight and edit its value, turn to change it, and push again to browse. From Chord, EXIT returns to Modes. From Granule or Vox, EXIT returns to Sequence Genie. From Sequence Setup, EXIT returns to Sound Generator.

While editing a number, hold SHIFT and turn VALUE for larger steps. Tempo, hits, rotation, velocity limits, gate, swing, strum, and Vox sensitivity move by 10 units. In Granule, grain size moves by 50 ms and the longer numeric ranges move by 10 units. Sample pad selection jumps by one bank. Menu choices and row navigation still move one at a time.

Piano roll

Turn VALUE while stopped to browse pages. On either Genie Suite build, push VALUE to enter step edit. Pads 1/2 choose Note, Chance, or Repeat. Pads 3/4 move the cursor. Turn VALUE to change the selected field. Pad 5 toggles hit or rest, and Pad 6 locks or unlocks the step. Chance runs from 0–100%. Repeat runs from 1–4 triggers. Push VALUE again to leave step edit. On Legacy, press Pad 15 to return to Setup for edits.

Bounce

Pad 11 captures one cycle from the first pass. ONCE saves with looping off. LOOP saves a forward loop. REV records reverse step order. PING records forward and back without doubling the turnaround steps. REV and PING save as regular forward-looping audio, with the change of direction already recorded. Looping bounces default to GATE off. The destination message names the pad, and its Original BPM uses the tempo at bounce launch.

The bounce uses the 404's internal recorder. The destination is routed outside Bus 1/2 to avoid processing the recorded signal twice. If levels differ, check bus processing and compression when comparing the two. Press EXIT to cancel a bounce or any remaining batch takes.

Load an instrument in DOOM OS 0.7.4

Hold REMAIN and press SUB PAD to open DOOM OS. On its step grid, hold SUB PAD, tap an empty pad, and release SUB PAD. Turn and press VALUE to pick the instrument. Return to Genie and select that instrument as its source. For DOOM OS controls, see the DOOM OS manual.

Chord Genie

On Modes, press Pad 1. Chord uses the current phrase, scale, and root. Select Sound Generator as the source for polyphonic chords.

  • Chord selects OFF, triad, seventh, suspended, or power.
  • Voicing selects Close, Open, Drop 2, or Spread.
  • Strum spaces chord notes by 0–120 ms. A new hit takes over from the preceding chord.
  • Prog Bars selects a repeating four- or eight-bar progression. Prog Reroll generates a new progression while preserving the phrase.

Pad 16 auditions, Pad 11 bounces, and Pad 15 or EXIT returns to Modes.

Granule Genie

On Modes, press Pad 2. Select a Source pad with VALUE. Press Pad 16 to acquire and play. Position and Spray set the location and its random spread. Scan moves the location forward or backward, with zero holding it in place. Size is 10–500 ms, Density is 1–32 grains per second, and Pitch Spread spans 0–3 octaves. Reverse is the chance of a backwards grain. Shape selects Hann, Saw, Perc, or Square. Stereo sets the pan spread.

Pad 6 adds a burst during playback. Pad 13 stops and restores the previous grain settings. Pad 15 toggles the grain scope. Pad 16 starts or stops. Pad 11 bounces one bar at the BPM set in Sequence Genie’s Tempo row after a source has loaded.

Vox Genie

On Modes, press Pad 4. Start with a vocal sample with clear gaps between syllables. Select its pad under Source, then press Pad 16. Vox finds attacks in the captured audio and plays the resulting chops as a stream. Use the 404’s regular Chop mode to place exact markers and assign individual slices to pads.

Troubleshooting

  • If Pad 15 switches only between Setup and Piano Roll, check the firmware you built. Both current Genie Suite options include Modes. Legacy and the earlier Stock + Sequence Genie build have Setup and Piano Roll.
  • If the source is unavailable, choose a populated pad and make sure it plays in normal Sample mode. For chopped vocals, try a clip with distinct transients.
  • If bounce reports REC BUSY / CHECK ROUTING, stop other recording or resampling, then retry. For batch bounce, check for a contiguous run of free pads.
  • If you hear a click or level jump when evaluating a bounce, check the compressor and compare with the same monitoring level.
Vox's screen, chops, playback, and bounce timing passed firmware emulation. Verify on the SP-404MKII by loading a vocal pad, viewing the chop marks, advancing through the chops, and bouncing to a spare pad.
