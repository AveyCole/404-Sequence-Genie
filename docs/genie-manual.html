Quick start

  1. Back up your projects and keep a known-good firmware pair. Put the original Roland 5.52 SP404MKII_APP0.bin and the generated SP404MKII_APP1.bin at the root of the update SD card.
  2. Insert the card. Hold SHIFT while powering on, then press VALUE at the update screen. Wait for UPDATE COMPLETE!, then power off, remove the card, and restart. Roland 5.52 update instructions
  3. Press SHIFT + RECORD SETTING to open Sound Generator. Press SHIFT + SUB PAD to enter Sequence Genie. On both Suite builds the lamp animation plays on entry. Press any numbered pad or turn VALUE to skip it and use that control. Press Pad 16 to audition. Watch the intro.
  4. On either Genie Suite build, press Pad 15 through Setup → Piano Roll → Modes. On Modes, choose Pad 1 for Chord, Pad 2 for Grain, Pad 3 for Drum, Pad 4 for Vox, or Pad 5 for Bounce. On Legacy, Pad 15 switches between Setup and Piano Roll.

Turn VALUE to browse rows. The highlighted label shows the selected row. Push to highlight and edit its value, turn to change it, and push again to browse. From Chord, Drum, or Bounce, EXIT returns to Modes. From Grain or Vox, EXIT returns to Sequence Genie. From Sequence Setup, EXIT returns to Sound Generator.

In Sequence Setup, SHIFT + VALUE jumps pages while browsing and makes coarse edits while editing. Sample pad selection jumps by one bank. Grain, Chord, Vox, and Bounce have mode-specific controls below.

Edit the next phrase while playing

On either Genie Suite, press SHIFT + Pad 12 during playback. When DRAFT appears, edit the next phrase in Setup, Piano Roll, or Drum Genie. Press Pad 12 to arm it for the next phrase boundary. When QUEUED appears, press Pad 12 again to resume editing or SHIFT + Pad 12 to discard the draft. If an ONCE phrase ends before you queue the draft, press Pad 16 to start it. When a draft is armed, the Setup shortcuts on Pads 5–10 wait until it is unqueued or launched.

Piano roll

Turn VALUE while stopped to browse pages. On either Genie Suite build, push VALUE to enter step edit. Pads 1/2 choose Note, Chance, Repeat, LEN, or TRIG. Pads 3/4 move the cursor. Turn VALUE to change the selected field. Pad 5 toggles hit or rest, and Pad 6 locks or unlocks the step. Chance runs from 0–100%. Repeat runs from 1–4 triggers. Push VALUE again to leave step edit. On Legacy, press Pad 15 to return to Setup for edits.

TRIG chooses which passes can play a hit. ALL allows every pass, 1ST allows only the first, and LATE allows every pass after the first. A fraction such as 2/4 allows only the second pass of each four-pass group. Chance applies on eligible passes. Ping-pong counts each direction as a pass. Starting playback or a bounce, or launching a queued phrase, starts the count over.

LEN can use AUTO (the phrase’s Gate Pct), 10–100% of one step or ratchet interval, or 2–8 steps. A multi-step note holds through rests until its length runs out, the next hit plays, or the playback pass ends. When a step ratchets, the earlier repeats use Gate Pct and the last repeat sustains. The roll draws the programmed lengths. Sample playback settings can affect how a pitched sample responds at note-off. LEN is included in undo, phrase slots, queued drafts, live MIDI, and audio bounce. Older phrase slots load with AUTO.

Drum Genie

Press Pad 7 for the drum step grid. Turn VALUE to move one step, or hold SHIFT and turn to jump 16 steps. Pads 1 and 2 select the lane. Push VALUE to add or mute a hit, and press Pad 8 to restore that step to Auto (Hits and Rotate). Pad 7 or EXIT returns to lane settings. Assign a source to the lane to hear its hits.

On the Modes page, press Pad 3. Turn VALUE to browse and push VALUE to edit. Select a lane in Drum Lane, then choose its Source. Sample plays the assigned pad. Tone plays the current Sound Generator tone at the selected note. Inst plays an instrument pad loaded in the current DOOM Suite project. MIDI sends the lane’s selected note on its own channel. Channel OFF silences that lane’s MIDI output. Pad 15 or EXIT returns to Modes while the phrase keeps playing.

A triangle marks the selected lane. The highlighted cell marks the parameter you’re editing. Squares are hits and dots are rests. An OFF lane has a blank strip and a dash count. An assigned lane with zero hits shows rests and a count of 0.

All eight lanes play together. The OLED shows four lanes at a time. In Drum Lane, turn past lane 4 to see lanes 5–8. Pads 1 and 2 also cross between those groups in the step grid.

The ruler and playhead follow the current 16-step bar. A draft shows its own first bar without a playhead. After you queue the draft, the overview follows the playing phrase until the handoff.

Drum Hits and Drum Rotate are independent of melodic Hits and Rotate. Ordinary drum samples ring out between steps. Looping drum pads restart at their next hit and stop at the end of playback.

Record a phrase to a sample pad

Pad 11 captures one cycle from the first pass. ONCE saves with looping off. LOOP saves a forward loop. REV records reverse step order. PING records forward and back without doubling the turnaround steps. REV and PING save as regular forward-looping audio, with the change of direction already recorded. Looping bounces default to GATE off. The destination message names the pad, and its Original BPM uses the tempo at bounce launch.

The bounce uses the 404's internal recorder. The destination is routed outside Bus 1/2 to avoid processing the recorded signal twice. If levels differ, check bus processing and compression when comparing the two. Press EXIT to cancel a bounce or any remaining batch takes.

Load an instrument in DOOM OS 0.7.4

Hold REMAIN and press SUB PAD to open DOOM OS. On its step grid, hold SUB PAD, tap an empty pad, and release SUB PAD. Turn and press VALUE to pick the instrument. Return to Genie and select that instrument as its source. For DOOM OS controls, see the DOOM OS manual.

Chord Genie

Press Pad 10 in Sequence Setup, or press Pad 1 from Modes. Chord uses the phrase’s scale and root. On Source, push and turn VALUE to switch from a sample or DOOM instrument to the Sound Generator for polyphonic chords.

  • Chord and Style choose the chord type and progression. FREE uses the seeded degree selection. POP, SAD, JAZZ, ROCK, and DRIFT choose progressions in the current scale.
  • Voicing offers Close, Open, Drop 2, Spread, and Smooth. Smooth uses the preceding chord to reduce leaps between its lower notes.
  • Rhythm offers Hit, Bar, Arp Up, Arp Down, and Arp Up/Down. Bar plays a chord on the first hit in a bar and melody notes on subsequent hits.
  • Strum MS and Strum Dir set 0–120 ms of spacing between chord notes, from low to high, high to low, or alternating. Bass adds Root or Low beneath the chord.
  • Prog Bars and Prog Reroll choose four or eight chord slots and reroll the progression while preserving the phrase.
  • Chord Steps sets how long each chord slot lasts. Choose 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, or 64 steps. At 4, a 16-step phrase can play four chords. Chords sound on phrase hits, so slots without hits stay silent.

Pads 5–8 select a bar on the displayed half. Turn VALUE or use Pads 3/4 to set its degree. Pad 10 restores AUTO. Pad 9 switches halves in eight-bar mode. Press the same bar pad, push VALUE, press Pads 1/2, or press EXIT to leave bar editing. Bar overrides are saved with phrase slots and work with undo.

Pad 16 plays or stops, Pad 11 records to a pad, and Pad 15 or EXIT returns to Modes from the main Chord screen.

Grain Genie

On Modes, press Pad 2. The waveform breaks into grains before the Source page opens. Choose a pad and a capture length of 1, 2, 4, or 8 seconds. Press Pad 16 to play. Grains start as soon as enough audio has loaded, and the dry source stays muted while the rest loads. Press Pad 16 again to cancel loading or stop playback.

Vox Genie

On Modes, press Pad 4. Source is selected first. Start with a vocal sample with clear gaps between syllables. Select its pad under Source, then press Pad 16. Vox finds attacks in the captured audio and plays the resulting chops as a stream. Use the 404’s regular Chop mode to place exact markers and assign individual slices to pads.

For continuous random chops with a scheduled jump every half note, set Order to RANDOM, enable a Sync division, set Hits to 2 and Rotate to 0, then choose FLOW. Vox uses Sequence Genie’s selected tempo source. The Sync division controls Pad 6 timing. Programmed hits use the sixteenth-note grid.

Vox silently captures up to eight seconds from the start of pad playback and downmixes it to mono. Capture ends earlier if the pad ends. Vox follows the source pad’s volume and peaks at about −24 dBFS on its dry mixer input at full pad volume. For a later vocal line, copy and trim that region to another pad first. Continuous audio may produce a single chop. Edits stop the current audition. Press Pad 16 to hear the new settings.

Bounce

On Modes, press Pad 5. BOX turns floor impacts into Sound Generator notes in the current scale. The left wall, right wall, and ceiling use Drum lanes 1–3. With COLLIDE enabled, ball collisions use lane 4. PLINKO sends balls through pegs into pitched bins.

Pad 11 records audio from the 404’s internal recorder.

On DOOM Suite, BOX Pads 4–6 keep their fast rhythms under long Sound Generator releases. When all four voices are busy, Bounce and Sequence Genie shorten the oldest Genie release tail to make room for the next note.

For steady quarter notes in BOX, set SYNC, bounciness to 100%, and time speed to 100%, then press Pad 2.

Both current Genie Suite builds add 12 custom stereo DSP effects directly into the SP-404MKII’s native MFX list and extend the grid to four pages (1/4–4/4, MFX LIST 49-64). Select them from the grid, VALUE encoder, or CTRL 3 knob just like stock effects. To jump directly to Page 4 while holding MFX, hold DJFX Looper + Isolator together.

Troubleshooting

  • If Pad 15 switches only between Setup and Piano Roll, check the firmware you built. Both current Genie Suite options include Modes. Legacy and the earlier Stock + Sequence Genie build have Setup and Piano Roll.
  • If the source is unavailable, choose a populated pad and make sure it plays in normal Sample mode. For chopped vocals, try a clip with distinct transients.
  • If bounce reports REC BUSY / CHECK ROUTING, stop other recording or resampling, then retry. For batch bounce, check for a contiguous run of free pads.
  • If you hear a click or level jump when evaluating a bounce, check the compressor and compare with the same monitoring level.
The Bounce intro, BOX timing, PLINKO note output, and CATCH/undo passed firmware emulation. Recorder and eight-ball audio-load checks still need the SP-404MKII.
